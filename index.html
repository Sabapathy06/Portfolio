Design for Manufacturing.
Engineered for Lifetime Reliability.

STRICT ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

All design data is authored in adherence to international drafting standards (ASME Y14.5, AWS D1.1, ANSI 92.2), ensuring exact tolerance stack-up and frictionless shop-floor assembly.

I am a Senior Mechanical Design Engineer with 8+ years of dedicated industry experience delivering robust mechanical systems, heavy vehicle structures, aerial equipment, and custom automation machinery. My engineering approach bridges conceptual physics and structural calculations with real-world shop floor manufacturability.

My core competency spans structural and sheet-metal design, large CAD assemblies, product development (NPD), sustaining engineering, and comprehensive manufacturing floor support. I implement rigorous DFM (Design for Manufacturing) and DFA (Design for Assembly) methodologies, GD&T per ASME Y14.5, Model-Based Definition (MBD), disciplined ECN/ECR workflows, and fundamental engineering calculations (bolt selection, force checks, stability analysis, and hydraulic schematics).

Having worked across multinational product teams (Terex India, Quest Global, Innoway Systems), I manage product lifecycles through modern PDM/PLM systems (Vault, Teamcenter), ensuring zero-defect documentation, seamless vendor handoffs, and sustainable field performance.
